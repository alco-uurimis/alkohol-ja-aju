# Usability test plan

Goal: validate that the site is understandable without verbal guidance.

## Participants
Recruit 5–8 people from the intended audience. Do not collect health information or alcohol histories during the test.

## Tasks
1. Find what research says about alcohol and sleep.
2. Find the limitation of one scientific claim.
3. Complete one mini-game and explain what its result does **not** measure.
4. Find the knowledge quiz and answer two items.
5. Find the research survey and explain, before opening it, what data it asks for.
6. Find where a 15–17-year-old can seek help in Estonia.
7. Find the methodology and data-policy pages.

## Observe
Record only usability observations: where navigation was unclear, wrong taps, dead ends, text that required rereading, and time-to-find. Do not record personal alcohol-related disclosures.

## Success criteria
- 7/8 participants find a science topic without help.
- 7/8 can locate a primary/source reference.
- 7/8 understand that mini-games are not sobriety/medical tests.
- 7/8 can find the survey and data policy.
- No critical mobile overflow or unreachable control.

## Follow-up
Prioritize repeated problems over individual preferences. Document changes in `changelog/`.
